# Specification amendments

Generated by `spec_diff.py`. The *specification* is the `requires` and `ensures` clauses of the functions that are not proof functions (executable functions, trait method declarations, `assume_specification`), and the spec functions they use, directly or through other spec functions. Comments are left out. Each item of the amended crate is matched by name with the same item of the original crate, and their specification lines are compared with a classic line-by-line diff. Hunk headers give the line numbers in the source files and end with the function's signature, as `diff -p` does. Every hunk is also in [`spec_diff.diff`](spec_diff.diff). Changes outside the specification (lemmas, proofs, imports, documentation, executable code) are counted, and listed under [Other changes](#other-changes-outside-the-specification), but not diffed.

A *clause* is one comma-separated expression after `requires` or `ensures`. Each item lists the clauses that the amendment adds or removes, compared token by token (up to the name of the result and trailing commas), with the kind the notes give them.

## Conclusions (from the notes)

What the developer's spec brought to Claude's first pass, judged on the diff of `src/`. The line and clause counts are from the tables below; which clauses are the developer's comes from comparing them with `verus_human_written_spec/` (see the notes on each item, and `verus_fixed/SPEC_REVIEW.md`).

- **The specification diff is small, and no clause of Claude's was wrong.** 109 lines are added and 5 removed, in `requires`/`ensures` clauses and five new spec functions. The 5 removed lines are preconditions, each replaced by a weaker one. 30 of the 33 added clauses are the developer's: most verbatim, the rest with `index_is` (H5) or other names. The other 3 are the weakened preconditions of `index` and `word_offset`.
- **Gaps closed: 11 clauses give callers a guarantee that Claude's contracts lacked.** These are frame conditions on the words already consumed: `next_word`, `next_u64_from_u32` (also the high half after a refill), `fill_bytes` and `reset_and_skip(0)`. Also new are the empty `fill_bytes` leaving the state unchanged, a contract for `UnwrapErr::re` (Claude's had none), `Ok` for an empty `dst` in `fill_bytes_via_next_word`, and the length clause of `TryRng::try_fill_bytes`/`Rng::fill_bytes` (a convenience). The findings behind them (SPEC_REVIEW.md C1, C4-C6) are each shown by a mutant or a client in `verus_fixed/verification/`.
- **Preconditions relaxed: 7 clauses, 4 of them the developer's.** `reconstruct`'s precondition rejected calls that are valid upstream, and Claude's report misstated upstream's behaviour. `reset_and_skip`'s made a cloned `BlockRng` unusable by verified code. The relaxations of `remaining_results` and `word_offset` (and of the private `index`, which they call) are marginal.
- **Restatements: 15 clauses** follow from Claude's `consumes`-based contracts. They add no guarantee, but they reconcile the two specs, and each is proved.
- **New spec functions: 5, all for the developer's vocabulary.** `spec_results` and `spec_from_le_bytes` are the developer's, verbatim. `index_is` is the developer's index predicate under a name that can serve as a trigger (H5).
- **Where the developer's spec was not followed.** Some of its preconditions are equivalent to Claude's: those of `new` (`0 < N <= W::spec_max()`), and those of `next_word`, `next_u64_from_u32` and `fill_bytes` (the closed `wf()`, by `lemma_wf_human`). Claude's are kept because they compose, which the developer's contracts do not (H1). `word_offset`'s `N > 0` is insufficient (H4). `read_words`'s precondition cannot be established by any caller (H2).
- **Cost, outside the specification: 27 items.** There are lemmas that bridge the two vocabularies (76 lines of lemma `requires`/`ensures`) and proofs (193 lines, 95 of them in `lemma_fill_bytes_human`). The trusted base gains one axiom (S7), needed only for the developer's `spec_from_le_bytes` on `u32`. The rest is imports and documentation. No executable line changed.
- Not in this diff: the two corrections to `VERIFICATION.md` (C7), and the review's experiments (`verus_fixed/SPEC_REVIEW.md`).
